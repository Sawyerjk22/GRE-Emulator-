Question 4 (Quantitative Comparison)
Problem Statement:
$k$ is an integer for which:
$$\frac{1}{2^{1-k}} < \frac{1}{8}$$
● Quantity A: $k$
● Quantity B: $-2$
Options:
● (A) Quantity A is greater.
● (B) Quantity B is greater.
● (C) The two quantities are equal.
● (D) The relationship cannot be determined from the information given.
Correct Answer: (B) Quantity B is greater.
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Note that $\frac{1}{8} = \frac{1}{2^3} = 2^{-3}$, and $\frac{1}{2^{1-k}} = 2^{-(1-k)} = 2^{k-
1}$.
2. The inequality becomes: $2^{k-1} < 2^{-3}$
3. Since the exponential base $2 > 1$, the exponent inequality preserves the order: $k - 1 < -3 \implies k
< -2$
4. Since $k$ is strictly less than $-2$, Quantity A ($k$) is strictly less than Quantity B ($-2$).
5. Therefore, Quantity B is greater.

Question 9 (Algebra / Exponents)
Problem Statement:
Which of the following is equal to $\frac{6^{14}}{(2^5)(3^7)}$?
Options:
● $(2^2)(3^2)$
● $(2^7)(3^7)$
● $(2^9)(3^2)$
● $(2^9)(3^7)$
● $(2^9)(3^9)$
Correct Answer: $(2^9)(3^7)$
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Express the base 6 in terms of its prime factors: $6 = 2 \times 3$.
2. Thus, $6^{14} = (2 \times 3)^{14} = 2^{14} \times 3^{14}$.
3. Divide by the denominator: $\frac{2^{14} \times 3^{14}}{2^5 \times 3^7} = 2^{14-5} \times 3^{14-
7} = 2^9 \times 3^7$.
Question 10 (Word Problem / Rates)
Problem Statement:
A certain train is traveling at a constant rate. If the train travels $s$ miles in $t$ hours, in how many hours will
the train travel $y$ miles?
Options:
● $\frac{sy}{t}$
● $\frac{st}{y}$
● $\frac{ty}{s}$
● $\frac{s}{ty}$
● $\frac{s+y}{t}$
Correct Answer: $\frac{ty}{s}$
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Speed (Rate) = $\frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{s}{t}\text{ mph}$.
2. Time needed to travel distance $y$ = $\frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Rate}} = \frac{y}{\frac{s}{t}} =
y \times \frac{t}{s} = \frac{ty}{s}\text{ hours}$.

Question 12 (Geometry)
Problem Statement:
In the figure shown, two lines intersect forming an angle $x^\circ$ between them, with outer angles $30^\circ$
and $40^\circ$ at the upper corners, and a right angle ($90^\circ$) at the bottom vertex. What is the value of
$x$?
Correct Answer: $160$
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. This figure is a non-convex (concave) quadrilateral.
2. By the exterior angle theorem, the interior non-reflex angle $x$ equals the sum of the three opposite
interior angles: $x = 30^\circ + 40^\circ + 90^\circ = 160^\circ$.
3. Alternatively, the four interior angles of the quadrilateral are $30^\circ, 40^\circ, 90^\circ$, and the
reflex angle $(360^\circ - x^\circ)$.
4. $\text{Sum} = 30^\circ + 40^\circ + 90^\circ + (360^\circ - x) = 360^\circ \implies 160^\circ +
360^\circ - x = 360^\circ \implies x = 160$.

Question 4 (Quantitative Comparison)
Problem Statement:
In triangle $IGK$:
● $H$ is the midpoint of $IG$
● $J$ is the midpoint of $IK$
● $IG = GK = 10$
● Quantity A: $IJ$
● Quantity B: $5$
Options:
● (A) Quantity A is greater.
● (B) Quantity B is greater.
● (C) The two quantities are equal.
● (D) The relationship cannot be determined from the information given.
Correct Answer: (D) The relationship cannot be determined from the information given.
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Since $J$ is the midpoint of $IK$, $IJ = \frac{1}{2} IK$.
2. By the Triangle Inequality theorem in $\triangle IGK$: $|IG - GK| < IK < IG + GK \implies |10 - 10|
< IK < 10 + 10 \implies 0 < IK < 20$.
3. Dividing by 2 gives: $0 < IJ < 10$.
4. If $IK = 10$, then $IJ = 5$ (A = B). If $IK = 14$, $IJ = 7$ (A > B). If $IK = 6$, $IJ = 3$ (B > A).
5. Therefore, the relationship cannot be determined.

Question 7 (Geometry)
Problem Statement:
The perimeter of square $S$ is 40. Square $T$ is inscribed in square $S$. What is the least possible area of
square $T$?
Options:
● 45
● 48
● 49
● 50
● 52
Correct Answer: 50
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Perimeter of $S = 40 \implies$ side length = 10.
2. Let $a$ and $10 - a$ be segments of the side of $S$ divided by a vertex of $T$.
3. $\text{Area}(T) = a^2 + (10 - a)^2 = 2a^2 - 20a + 100$.
4. The minimum value occurs when $a = 5$ (midpoint): $\text{Area}_{\min} = 2(5)^2 - 20(5) + 100 =
50 - 100 + 100 = 50$.

Question 15 (Inequalities / Positive Numbers)
Problem Statement:
If $x, y, z > 0$ and $3x < 2y < 4z$, which could be true?
Options:
● $x = y$, $y = z$, $y > z$, $x > z$
Correct Answer: $y = z$, $y > z$, $x > z$
Step-by-Step Solution:
1. $x = y$: $3x < 2x \implies 3 < 2$ (False).
2. $y = z$: Let $y=1, z=1 \implies 2 < 4$. If $x=0.5, 1.5 < 2 < 4$ (True).
3. $y > z$: Let $z=1, y=1.5 \implies 3 < 4$. If $x=0.5, 1.5 < 3 < 4$ (True).
4. $x > z$: Let $z=1, x=1.1, y=1.8 \implies 3.3 < 3.6 < 4$ (True).
